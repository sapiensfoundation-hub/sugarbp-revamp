Tips to protect your kidneys

None of these require a prescription. Together, they're the strongest everyday defence against chronic kidney disease — whether or not you already have hypertension or diabetes.

🧊

Reduce salt in your diet

Lower sodium intake eases the load on your kidneys and blood pressure. Reducing salt by a third in the average diet is estimated to cut heart attack and stroke risk substantially.

🏃

Exercise 30 minutes daily

Regular activity helps regulate both blood pressure and blood sugar over the long term.

🥗

Eat a balanced diet

Favour whole foods over processed ones, and keep portions sensible.

⚕️

Keep your weight under control

Excess weight is a direct driver of both hypertension and diabetes. See your BMI & waist-hip ratio.

💊

Avoid long-term non-prescription drugs

Certain painkillers and unregulated "tonics," taken over years, quietly damage kidney tissue.

🚬

Stop smoking

Smoking accelerates the decline of kidney function in people already at risk.

❤️

Check your BP once a year

Hypertension has no symptoms until damage is already done — see the BP thresholds.

🧞

Check urine for albumin once a year

Albumin in urine is often the earliest detectable sign of CKD, appearing long before other symptoms.

🩸

Check your blood sugar once a year

Catching pre-diabetes early can prevent kidney complications entirely — see the sugar thresholds.
