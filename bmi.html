Obesity

BMI & waist-hip ratio

Weight alone can't tell you whether you're carrying healthy or unhealthy mass — these two measurements adjust for body size and fat distribution.

What is Body Mass Index (BMI)?

BMI is a measure of obesity. Weight alone cannot diagnose obesity, since it doesn't take a person's height into consideration.

BMI = Weight (kg) / Height² (m)
Weight in kilograms divided by height in metres, squared
CategoryBMI range
Underweight<18.5
Normal18.5–25
Overweight25–30
Obese30–40
Very obese>40

What is waist-hip ratio (WHR)?

Waist-hip ratio is a better index than BMI for predicting cardiovascular and chronic kidney disease risk, because it captures where fat is stored rather than just how much of it there is.

The waist is measured with a tape just below the last rib and above the belly button (umbilicus). The hip is measured at the widest bony point over the pelvis.

Women0.7>0.9
Men<0.9>1.0
