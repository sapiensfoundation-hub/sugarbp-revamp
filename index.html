Chronic kidney disease shows no early warning

Your kidneys fail silently. Catch it before they do.

High blood pressure and diabetes are the two leading causes of chronic kidney disease worldwide. The body can adapt until ninety percent of kidney function is gone — which is why most people feel nothing until the damage is already done. SugarBP exists to help you check the numbers that matter, on a schedule, not on symptoms.

Why CKD goes unnoticed

Chronic kidney disease has no early symptoms

The kidneys have enormous reserve capacity — they keep filtering blood even after most of their function is gone. That's why CKD is usually found late, and why testing on a schedule, not on symptoms, is the only reliable defence.
